One opinion, no range

Why now

Four shifts landed in the last 18 months

01  Prediction became nearly free

Sentinel-2 revisits every ~5 days, VIIRS maps lights nightly, GloFAS models every river, all open. Valuing and risk-checking a property from space now costs paise.

02  Land data became infrastructure

ULPIN on 36 crore parcels; banks in 406 districts verify mortgages online; land records are being wired into the Unified Lending Interface; PM GatiShakti opened 230 datasets.

03  Valuations now travel between lenders

Co-lending directions cover all regulated lenders from 1 Jan 2026; securitisation hit a record ₹2.55 lakh crore in FY26, NBFC-led. Every hand-over of a loan re-opens its valuation.

04  Regulators want explainable AI

RBI's FREE-AI framework (Aug 2025) and the DPDP Rules (full compliance by May 2027) reward models that log sources, explain every number and keep humans in charge.

JUDGMENT · NBFC

How much to lend?

LTV policy, exceptions, final sanction. The valuer still visits.

Confirm

Our range agrees with the valuer and no flag fires: the file moves without a second review.

Verify

One flag: the valuer gets a checklist for exactly that issue, not a generic re-visit.

Refer

Value outside range, below the government floor, or high hazard: second valuation and legal review.

The AI edge. Each model gets better with every file: valuer overrides and auction outcomes retrain the value, liquidity and haircut models per district. A competitor can copy the screens, not the labels.

Why explainable, not black box. Lenders must justify every decision to the RBI, so weights are statistical and visible.

Five steps, four of them AI, each named for what it does. Field visits stay for now: ClarityX's founders were clear that lenders will use this as a complementary check until the regulator changes the rules, as happened with credit scores. The value is in routing reviews, not removing the valuer.

Learned weights, still readable

Spatial scores in this market are typically fixed-weight indices (e.g. 50% price trend, 30% liquidity, 10% growth, 10% access).MR We fit weights to realised recoveries per district, and publish them per report.

Guard against biased data

Approved loans and auctioned assets are not random. We track a sample of rejected files and hold out whole districts and later months.

Honest about today

The prototype runs live on public data with transparent priors. Calibration on a design partner's files is milestone one.

MR = market research (industry materials, Nov 2026; not publicly verified). Targets are BhoomiPulse pilot goals. Conformal intervals: the 80% range must contain the true sale price 80% of the time on held-out files.
Why the regression and not a deep network: the ClarityX founders told us a black-box model may not be accepted by lenders who must justify decisions to the RBI. We get accuracy from data, and keep the model readable.

Proof

A 90-day pilot designed to prove cause, not correlation

WEEKS 0–4 · BACK-TEST

Out-of-time, out-of-district

24 months of closed files: our value vs the valuer's vs the later sale or auction price; a reject sample to correct selection bias.

WEEKS 5–12 · RANDOMISED

Branches randomised to the second check

Treatment branches route reviews confirm / verify / refer; control branches keep today's process. A random 10% of confirmed files still get a second valuation.

ALSO · THRESHOLD DESIGN

Just above vs just below the cut-off

Files either side of the confirm cut-off are near-identical, so outcome differences isolate the effect of the routing itself.

Go / no-go metricTargetGo / no-go metricTarget

A before-and-after comparison would confuse our effect with seasonality. Branch randomisation plus a threshold design gives the risk committee a causal estimate. DPD means days past due: 30+ DPD is the first sign a loan is going bad.
