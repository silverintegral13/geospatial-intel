Why now

Four shifts landed in the last 18 months

01  Prediction became nearly free

Sentinel-2 revisits every ~5 days, VIIRS maps lights nightly, GloFAS models every river since 1984; all open. The cost of predicting value, hazard and activity per asset has collapsed to paise.

02  Land data became infrastructure

ULPIN on 36 crore parcels; banks in 406 districts verify mortgages online; land records are being wired into the Unified Lending Interface; PM GatiShakti opened 230 datasets to the private sector.

03  Capital now flows between lenders

Co-lending directions cover all regulated entities from 1 Jan 2026; securitisation hit a record ₹2.55 lakh crore in FY26, NBFC-led. Every handover of a loan needs an independent view of its collateral.

04  Regulators want auditable AI

RBI's FREE-AI framework (Aug 2025: 7 sutras, 26 recommendations) and the DPDP Rules (full compliance by May 2027) reward models that log sources, explain scores and keep humans in charge.

Sources: Govt of India / DoLR (ULPIN, online mortgage verification, Nov 2025–Apr 2026; ULI integration, Jul 2026); DPIIT/BISAG-N (PM GatiShakti Public, Oct 2025); RBI Co-Lending Arrangements Directions (6 Aug 2025); Crisil Ratings (securitisation FY26); RBI FREE-AI Committee Report (13 Aug 2025); MeitY DPDP Rules (13 Nov 2025).
Why this is a now business and not a 2015 business: free data, digital public infrastructure for land, capital moving across lenders, and a regulator that has written down what responsible AI in credit looks like.
The economics

AI makes the prediction cheap. The lender keeps the judgment, and redesigns the workflow around it

PREDICTION · AI

What is it worth?

P10–P50–P90 value, rate per sq ft or acre, declared-value check.

PREDICTION · AI

What will it fetch?

Days to sell, forced-sale haircut, realisable value after carrying costs.

PREDICTION · AI

What could go wrong?

Flood, heat, drought, access, land-use mismatch, activity drop.

JUDGMENT · LENDER

How much to lend?

LTV policy, risk appetite, exceptions, social objectives, final sanction.

Fast-track

Clean, liquid, low-hazard assets get a desktop valuation the same day.

Verify

One flag: a targeted site visit on exactly that issue, not a generic one.

Refer

Multiple flags or weak collateral: full field valuation and legal review.

The gain is not a better number in the old process. It is a new process: valuers stop visiting the easy 40–50% of files and spend their time on the hard ones, where human judgment earns its fee.

This is the decision-decomposition view: cheap prediction makes judgment more valuable. Enterprise ROI from AI depends on workflow redesign, so we sell a routing decision (fast-track, verify, refer), not just a score.
The product

One engine, three products, one API

Collateral Reportat origination

Drop a pin or trace the parcel. In about 8 seconds: grade A–E, value range, realisable value, days to sell, hazards, land-use check, LTV band under the lender's own policy, red flags and a routing decision, with every source logged.

Pulseafter disbursal

Monthly re-check of each financed asset from orbit: factory night lights, crop vigour from Sentinel-2, river flood forecasts. Signed webhooks push alerts to collections before EMIs bounce.

Investor Lensbetween lenders

Upload a data tape; get a book-level collateral certificate: realisable cover, under-covered exposure, district concentration, flood exposure, stress tests for price shocks and monsoon floods.

API + widgeteverywhere

REST API on the edge or on-prem Docker, per-lender credit policy, Python and JS SDKs, an embeddable card for loan-origination screens, and an audit log for model-risk teams.

Guard against biased training data

Approved loans and auctioned assets are not a random sample. We track a sample of rejected files, weight auction data to the lender's full collateral population, and hold out whole districts and later months.

Honest about today

The prototype runs live on public data with transparent priors. Calibration on a design partner's files and recoveries is the first milestone, and every response carries its model version.

The professor's challenge: how is your algorithm better? Our answer is a measurable protocol with targets, out-of-time and out-of-geography tests, conformal intervals so the range means what it says, and explicit fixes for selection bias in lending data.

Proof

A 90-day pilot designed to prove cause, not correlation

WEEKS 0–4 · BACK-TEST

Out-of-time, out-of-district

24 months of closed files: our value vs the valuer's and vs later sale or auction price; default uplift on loans originated after the training window; a reject sample to correct selection bias.

WEEKS 5–12 · RANDOMISED

Branches randomised to AI triage

Treatment branches route files fast-track / verify / refer; control branches keep today's process. A random 10% of fast-tracked files still get a full valuation to measure error safely.

ALSO · THRESHOLD DESIGN

Compare just above and below the cut-off

Files just either side of the fast-track score cut-off are near-identical, so differences in outcomes isolate the effect of the routing itself.

Responsible by design

Built to pass the model-risk committee

Scores describe land, not people

No caste, religion, gender or community variable, enforced by test. Activity and hazard measure the place. Outcomes are monitored by region and asset type.

Humans decide

AI predicts and routes; credit officers sanction. Every response carries sources, latency, model version and a plain-language rationale; the audit log answers "why this LTV?".

FREE-AI ready

Model cards, versioning, drift monitoring and an audit trail map to RBI's FREE-AI recommendations; outsourcing clauses and incident reporting come standard.

DPDP by architecture

Public-data scoring needs no personal data. Land records and cash flows come only through consent (ULI, Account Aggregator). On-prem deployment keeps data in the lender's network.

No common pricing hub

One vendor scoring many lenders could align their behaviour. Each tenant gets its own policy and calibration; we never recommend interest rates or share one lender's signals with another.

Augment the valuer

Valuers become exception handlers on flagged files. Junior officers and new branches gain most from a consistent second opinion.

Sources: RBI FREE-AI Committee Report (Aug 2025); MeitY DPDP Rules 2025; RBI Co-Lending Arrangements Directions 2025.
Covers fairness, accountability, regulation, competition policy and labour. The common-hub point matters: if every lender runs the same model, their decisions correlate. Tenant-specific calibration and no rate recommendations keep competition intact.

Risks

What could kill this, and what we do about it

Valuations not accurate enoughhigh

Calibrate per district on partner outcomes; ship ranges, not points; route uncertain files to humans. Kill criterion: MdAPE above 20% after calibration.

Long enterprise sales cycleshigh

Land with a data-tape Pulse audit that needs no integration; free 90-day pilot; sell through LOS and co-lending platforms.

Regulators or valuers push backmedium

Position as triage and a second opinion; statutory valuations stay with registered valuers; full audit trail.

Public data access changesmedium

Every connector is swappable and cached; self-hosted map stacks; paid imagery as fallback; multiple sources per signal.

Incumbents copy the featuresmedium

Features copy, outcome data does not. Recovery labels and workflow embedding compound with every tenant.

Data protection breachmedium

No personal data needed for scoring; consent-only connectors; on-prem option; ISO 27001 and SOC 2 roadmap.

Be explicit about the kill criterion for the core model. That builds credibility with a lender's risk committee and with investors.
